01 / THE REASON IT MATTERED
♥

For the girl who made
this Pujo feel like magic.

After we got together, this was our first Pujo that we got to celebrate side by side. We began on Mahalaya, at Kumortuli, surrounded by beginnings and the quiet excitement of a city waking up to its favourite time of year.

Then came the lights, the pandals, the food, the long walks, and all the little moments that somehow became the biggest ones. I knew Pujo was important to you. Getting to be part of it with you made it important to me, too.

I miss you. And I would relive every bit of it with you. ♡

P.S. THERE'S SOMETHING FOR YOU

A little letter, just for you.

Some things are better opened slowly.

My dear Pyancha,

I don't think I can explain how much this Pujo meant to me. I knew it was special to you, and getting to be beside you through it made every little thing feel special to me too.

You remembered Maddox Square because you remembered what I wanted. That tiny thing said so much. I keep thinking about the lights, the pandals, the food, the walking around — but mostly, I keep thinking about how it all felt because it was with you.

I miss you more than these words can say. If I could, I'd go back and walk every road again, stand in every crowd again, and take every slightly blurry picture again — as long as it was with you.

Thank you for making our first Pujo ours.

Always,
your Dino ♡
